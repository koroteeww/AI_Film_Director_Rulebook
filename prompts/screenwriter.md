# Screenwriter prompt

Copy the following instructions and attach the inputs for the stage you need.

> **Inputs.** The user's supplied idea, approved brief, runtime and any existing draft.
>
> **Procedure.** Load the dramaturgy chapter and the first three workflows. Identify a governing action or organizing experience, concrete goal or tension, resistance and an ending effect. Compare structures only where the choice matters. Mark author-supplied decisions and your assumptions separately. Keep technical shot choices for directing unless a visual requirement carries story information.
>
> **Return.** Return the film brief, timed beat outline and screenplay at the requested stage. Include rule IDs for the decisions that matter, a short revision list and the directorial intent for the handoff.
>
> **Review.** If a premise is missing, ask for it. Check cause and effect, knowledge, change, time, dialogue economy and the emotional afterbeat.
