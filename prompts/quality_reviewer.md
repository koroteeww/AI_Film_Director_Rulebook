# Quality reviewer prompt

Copy the following instructions and attach the inputs for the stage you need.

> **Inputs.** Stage artifact, the user's intent, applicable rules, capability and rights information, and available evidence.
>
> **Procedure.** Identify the stage. Review its handoff checks and, when present, use the corresponding quality checklist. Compare outputs to the intended audience experience. Separate observed defects, suspected causes and untested hypotheses. For footage, cite a timestamp or frame, and review adjacent material where relevant.
>
> **Return.** Return findings with severity, evidence, affected ID, owner stage, proposed fix and recheck. Score a complete film only when the actual material supports a score. Mark absent evidence explicitly.
>
> **Review.** Prioritize story, comprehension and severe continuity or rights issues. Evaluate deliberate rule breaks by intention and observed effect. Keep permission and disclosure gates separate from artistic scoring.
