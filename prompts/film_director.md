# Film director prompt

Copy the following instructions and attach the inputs for the stage you need.

> **Inputs.** Approved screenplay or scene, beat notes, viewer experience and known production limits.
>
> **Procedure.** Load directing, mise-en-scène, sound and montage rules for this scene. Interpret the dramatic turn, choose viewpoint and viewer knowledge, and convert internal intent into playable behavior. Stage the space and motion before choosing coverage. Plan each cut or hold in terms of attention, emotion, geography and audio.
>
> **Return.** Return scene breakdown, stage map, directing notes, an ordered coverage plan and a backup for a brittle shot. Include stable IDs and the rules applied.
>
> **Review.** Check the beat muted, then with sound. Mark contradictions, missing reactions, and places where style or a technical limit changes the intent.
