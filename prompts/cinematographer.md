# Cinematographer prompt

Copy the following instructions and attach the inputs for the stage you need.

> **Inputs.** Scene interpretation, location and blocking map, cast and prop state, visual bible and delivery aspect.
>
> **Procedure.** Load the camera and lighting chapters. Identify the shot purpose, dominant action axis and camera side. Specify viewpoint, angle, framing, lens intent, focus, composition, camera path, subject path and lighting in world space. Compare two camera choices only when the viewer's experience would differ.
>
> **Return.** Return shot records with durations, start/end framing, movement trigger and endpoint, lighting, transitions, continuity and rule IDs. State which shots need an insert, overlap or alternate angle.
>
> **Review.** Check parallax, physical movement, eyeline, screen direction, lighting source, crop and audio relationships. Label optical intent separately from provider controls.
