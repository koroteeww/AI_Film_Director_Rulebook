# 28 starter rules

Start with the decisions that change the user's short most. Load the full domain and source notes when a check fails. The order follows the workflow. Rule priority in the catalog is a separate field.

| Stage | Rule | First check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story | [DRA-001 · Unify the action](../rulebooks/01_dramaturgy.md#dra-001) | Remove one scene on paper. Keep it if a later cause, emotional interpretation or significant pattern changes. |
| Story | [DRA-002 · Link decisions and consequences](../rulebooks/01_dramaturgy.md#dra-002) | A reader can connect the resolution to an earlier condition or choice without inventing a new event. |
| Story | [DRA-003 · Separate goal, motive, and need](../rulebooks/01_dramaturgy.md#dra-003) | Someone can identify success or failure from a planned action and explain why it matters to this person. |
| Story | [DRA-005 · Turn the scene](../rulebooks/01_dramaturgy.md#dra-005) | Read the entry and exit states aloud. If the distinction is hard to name, check the scene's purpose or combine it. |
| Story | [DRA-008 · Pay off what the film emphasizes](../rulebooks/01_dramaturgy.md#dra-008) | Every strongly emphasized setup has a later function or an explicit reason to stay open. |
| Story | [DRA-015 · Compress between consequential beats](../rulebooks/01_dramaturgy.md#dra-015) | A timed read or animatic fits with space to understand the critical turn. Test every omitted link for ambiguity. |
| Story | [DRA-017 · Land the consequence and the afterbeat](../rulebooks/01_dramaturgy.md#dra-017) | After a read or animatic, ask what happened, what is still uncertain, and what the last beat made the viewer feel. |
| Direction | [DIR-001 · Name the intended viewer experience](../rulebooks/02_directing.md#dir-001) | Each major shot or cue can name a contribution. Remove or revise choices with conflicting purposes. |
| Direction | [DIR-003 · Direct with a playable action](../rulebooks/02_directing.md#dir-003) | A viewer can describe something the performer tries to do and where their attention is directed. |
| Direction | [DIR-006 · Convert performance notes into observable behavior](../rulebooks/02_directing.md#dir-006) | Check in context at intended size and speed. The cue should be legible and leave the next shot in a compatible state. |
| Direction | [DIR-010 · Give the camera and cut a reason](../rulebooks/02_directing.md#dir-010) | Compare the shot to a locked version. Keep the move when it adds the intended information, relation or experience. |
| Camera | [CIN-CAM-004 · Track the action axis and screen direction](../rulebooks/03_cinematography.md#cin-cam-004) | Draw both camera positions against the axis and compare the adjacent rendered frames. |
| Camera | [CIN-CAM-005 · Match eyelines and reverse angles](../rulebooks/03_cinematography.md#cin-cam-005) | Pause both shots and trace their lines of sight. They should meet the planned target in the shared map. |
| Camera | [CIN-CAM-007 · Match action across a cut](../rulebooks/03_cinematography.md#cin-cam-007) | Inspect two frames around the cut and play at speed. Check for a duplicated, skipped or reversed action. |
| Camera | [CIN-CAM-008 · Compose a readable visual hierarchy](../rulebooks/03_cinematography.md#cin-cam-008) | View the frame small. The intended emphasis survives crop, contrast and motion. |
| Camera | [CIN-CAM-010 · Set perspective with camera position](../rulebooks/03_cinematography.md#cin-cam-010) | Check relative sizes and parallax against the plan. Confirm that matched framing has the intended perspective. |
| Camera | [CIN-CAM-017 · Time movement to a beat and protect the endpoint](../rulebooks/03_cinematography.md#cin-cam-017) | Scrub start, trigger and end, then verify the intended emphasis and cut in the assembled timeline. |
| Staging | [MIS-001 · Map the playable space](../rulebooks/04_mise_en_scene.md#mis-001) | Trace every route, look and camera from the same map. |
| Staging | [MIS-007 · Treat props as stateful action objects](../rulebooks/04_mise_en_scene.md#mis-007) | Every important state transition has an observable cause or intentional ellipsis. |
| Staging | [MIS-012 · Carry scene state through the visual bible](../rulebooks/04_mise_en_scene.md#mis-012) | Compare the expected and observed state at each join. Reject unintended changes that affect identity, geography or causal action. |
| Light and color | [LGT-001 · Motivate the visible light](../rulebooks/05_lighting_and_color.md#lgt-001) | Across cuts, trace shadow and highlight direction to the mapped source or documented stylization. |
| Light and color | [LGT-010 · Protect separation and exposure continuity](../rulebooks/05_lighting_and_color.md#lgt-010) | Compare adjacent frames with stills or appropriate scopes and play the cut at speed. |
| Sound | [SND-002 · Protect intelligibility where speech carries meaning](../rulebooks/06_sound_design.md#snd-002) | Listen at a modest level with eyes closed and confirm the needed words and intent can be understood. |
| Sound | [SND-004 · Lay a continuous scene ambience](../rulebooks/06_sound_design.md#snd-004) | Listen over joins without picture and check for gaps, repeated ticks, sudden changes and level pumping. |
| Sound | [SND-009 · Bridge picture cuts with independently timed sound](../rulebooks/06_sound_design.md#snd-009) | Watch and listen through the transition. Identify which scene each audible cue belongs to and whether the overlap lands cleanly. |
| Edit | [EDT-001 · Orient the viewer through the sequence](../rulebooks/07_montage_and_editing.md#edt-001) | A first viewer can follow who did what, to whom, and when. |
| Edit | [EDT-003 · Rank emotion, story, and rhythm before cosmetic repair](../rulebooks/07_montage_and_editing.md#edt-003) | Show both versions and ask which better carries the intended feeling and story information. |
| Edit | [EDT-009 · Use ellipsis to compress time](../rulebooks/07_montage_and_editing.md#edt-009) | A first viewer understands the time jump, the causal relation and the consequence. |
