# M01 · Russian and Soviet traditions

The source trail has three distinct lines. Training in dramatic action starts with actor and script work. Soviet montage theory asks how selection and juxtaposition shape meaning. Tarkovsky's writing provides a separate route into duration, rhythm and attention within the shot. Later chapters will compare these choices at the scene level.

## Acting and dramaturgy

- **Константин Станиславский.** The chapter on action, «если бы» and предлагаемые обстоятельства gives a route from the given situation to something the performer can do. A future motion prompt can preserve the situation in its handoff and express the action as observable behavior. That translation is a rulebook inference. [SRC-STAN-ACT](../sources/bibliography.md#src-stan-act)
- **Михаил Чехов.** His acting tradition uses imagined embodiment and психологический жест to explore a character. In production planning, a rehearsal gesture can help the director select a small visible adjustment. The practitioner guide explains the technique, and the Routledge record identifies the book. [SRC-MCHEK-TECH](../sources/bibliography.md#src-mchek-tech) · [SRC-MCHEK-ACTOR](../sources/bibliography.md#src-mchek-actor)
- **Антон Чехов.** The 1889 letter's dramatic promise concerns a conspicuous monologue. In Act IV of «Вишневый сад», Varya and Lopakhin spend their opportunity to speak on departures and mundane details. One reading is that their behavior makes a consequential omission legible. The recurring broken-string cue has a different function from a plot mechanism. [SRC-CHEK-GUN](../sources/bibliography.md#src-chek-gun) · [SRC-CHEK-CHR](../sources/bibliography.md#src-chek-chr) · [SRC-CHEK-PLAY](../sources/bibliography.md#src-chek-play)

## Working vocabulary

| Russian | English gloss | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| предлагаемые обстоятельства | given circumstances | Conditions of the role or situation that make an action intelligible |
| действие | action | What the performer does to pursue a purpose, including active listening or refusal |
| сверхзадача | super-objective | Larger organizing purpose across a role or work |
| сквозное действие | through-line of action | Coherent pursuit across the work |
| психологический жест | psychological gesture | Michael Chekhov's actor-training concept |
| подтекст | subtext | What action and speech imply without stating it directly |
| мизансцена | staging, or arrangement within the scene | Often narrower than film-theory uses of mise-en-scène that include design and lighting |
| кадр | shot or frame | Resolve whether it means a time span in the edit or a single image |
| монтаж | editing or montage | Can mean general assembly or a specific expressive theory or sequence |
| эффект Кулешова | Kuleshov effect | A contextual interpretation effect with an empirical and historical research trail |
| кино-глаз | Kino-Eye | Vertov's historically situated program of cinematic observation and construction |

Include the original term when the translation could change the decision. Map кадр to a specific schema object before handing a request to the frame or motion stage.
