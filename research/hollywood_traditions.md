# M01 · Hollywood and international craft traditions

Field's feature-screenplay paradigm, an AFI directing exercise, and a Japanese director's account of film production answer different questions. Use each for the decision it addresses.

Three questions guide the dramaturgy milestone. What can the viewer infer from the causal chain? What choice reveals the character? What emotion should the audience carry out of the film? A structure may answer one of these better than another. Compare options before writing a scene.

## From script to a filmed event

- Hitchcock's AFI examples suggest looking for actions that reveal information and for actions made possible by the setting itself. The auction-room escape tactic depends on the rules of the space. [SRC-HITCH-AFI](../sources/bibliography.md#src-hitch-afi)
- Weston gives an actor-facing route into performance and rehearsal. Lumet's professional memoir and Kurosawa's translated memoir connect preparation to production choices. Mamet's directing lectures offer a route into constructing meaning across shots. The register distinguishes catalogues and accessible passages. [Weston](../sources/bibliography.md#src-west-act) · [Lumet](../sources/bibliography.md#src-lum-mak) · [Kurosawa](../sources/bibliography.md#src-kuro-auto) · [Mamet](../sources/bibliography.md#src-mam-dir)
- Bordwell, Thompson and Smith provide analytical categories for studying technique. Block supplies an excerpt on visual contrast and affinity. Brown, Alton, ASC and Chion are starting points for connecting optical, lighting, and sound decisions to what the audience perceives. [Film Art](../sources/bibliography.md#src-bord-form) · [Block](../sources/bibliography.md#src-block-vis) · [Image and sound](../sources/bibliography.md#image-and-sound)

## Transfer to production

Choose a scene result that a viewer can recognize. Let actors pursue an objective within the space. Assign each shot or sound cue a purpose such as revealing information, withholding it, connecting a reaction to its cause, or changing emotional emphasis. Diagnose problems in a rough cut before changing style or asking a generator for more complex output. Test these methods in the domain milestones.
