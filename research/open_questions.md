# Open research questions

The research milestones are complete. The following items affect particular production decisions or future source updates.

| Question | What was established | What to verify when needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which passages in restricted books justify a precise claim? | Catalogue and indexed entries identify several works. Accessible primary texts, interviews and scoped excerpts support the published rules. | Consult the relevant edition when adding a claim about a passage that was unavailable, particularly for Kuleshov, Mamet, The Film Sense and detailed book-only claims. |
| How reliable are exact endpoint, beat and timestamp instructions? | [Seedance research](seedance_research.md) distinguishes provider controls and source evidence from performance. | Run the documented small timing and continuity test in the actual provider, model, reference mode and account. Review the cut before scaling. |
| What works in a particular CapCut build? | The [platform guide](../ai_production/capcut_workflow.md) records public feature evidence and manual alternatives. | Verify controls and a short export on the actual device and intended platform. |
| How well does a repair generalize across generations? | The [failure matrix](../ai_production/failure_modes.md) gives observable faults and plausible interventions. | Log the inputs and candidate outputs, change one variable where practical, and recheck adjacent shots. |
| Which release permissions, venue conditions and disclosure methods apply? | The [rights chapter](../quality/rights_and_disclosure.md) records dated examples and a decision ledger. | Use the current edition, jurisdiction, account terms and intended distribution. Verify the actual export and upload when marking or captions matter. |

Open questions are evidence or production checks. Record new results with a source ID, exact scope and update date.
