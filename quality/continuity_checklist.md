# Continuity checklist

For each join record outgoing and incoming shot/clip IDs, usable trims, intended relation, planned exception if any, result, issue owner and recheck status.

## Before generation

- [ ] Script beat and emotional direction match the shot purpose.
- [ ] Current visual-bible versions are recorded for location, character, costume, props, light and look.
- [ ] Camera side, action axis, eyelines, depth and paths agree with the map.
- [ ] First and end frame roles and physical transitions agree with the actual provider mode.
- [ ] Prop contact, hand, pose, position and condition have a before/after state.
- [ ] Sound source, listener, sync event, planned bed and handoff are named.
- [ ] The intended clip length and editable handles fit the documented mode.

## Inspect footage and each cut

- [ ] Compare intended and observed beginning, key action, reaction and ending. Inspect enough intermediate frames to catch drift.
- [ ] Compare facial identity, costume, silhouette, prop state, screen direction, eyelines, contact and geometry across angles.
- [ ] Match source direction, shadow quality, exposure, palette and atmosphere, or verify the planned change.
- [ ] Play the cut at speed for action phase, viewability, emotional timing and unintended camera motion.
- [ ] Listen through the join for voice, room tone, perspective, speech sync, overlap and silence.
- [ ] Record an intentional discontinuity with the viewer effect and the cue that supports it. Review it in context.
- [ ] Resolve, repair or accept each discrepancy with timecode and rationale. Reopen affected neighbors when the trim or take changes.

Use the [failure table](../ai_production/failure_modes.md) for repair and the [continuity ledger](../ai_production/continuity_management.md) for approved state.
