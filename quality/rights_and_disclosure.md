# Rights, consent and AI disclosure

This reference records selected laws, agency analysis, venue rules and provider terms checked on 2026-11-06. Decide which jurisdictions and terms apply to the actual production and release. Keep contract, consent and identity evidence in access-controlled storage. The public project can refer to private records by ID.

## Legal and license checks

| Topic | Source and scope | Production record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human authorship and originality | The U.S. Copyright Office distinguishes sufficient human expressive authorship, including some creative arrangement or modification, from merely providing prompts. Kazakhstan's AI law also addresses human creative contribution in Article 23. | Record the user's idea and human writing, framing choices, selection, arrangement, editing, design and modifications. Track asset origin and compare borrowed or reference material against intended use. Assess protection and third-party clearance separately. [U.S. Copyright Office](../sources/bibliography.md#src-usco-ai2) · [Kazakhstan law](../sources/bibliography.md#src-kz-ai-cons) |
| Likeness, voice and consent | Kazakhstan's Civil Code Article 145 protects use of a person's image and contains exceptions. Other rights in a replica, voice, personal data or performance depend on applicable law and agreements. | For an identifiable person, record who authorized the relevant input, synthetic changes, voice or image use, publication, territories, term, media and festival/promotional use. Resolve scope before generation and release. [Gov.kz explanation of Article 145](../sources/bibliography.md#src-kz-image) |
| Music | A film can use distinct rights in a composition and a particular recording. Platform music may have limits. The posted CapCut agreement names CapCut, TikTok and TikTok for Business for its described Commercial Sounds use and calls for separate permission beyond that scope. | Record track, composer, recording, rights holder, cue time and use, license, distribution media, term, territories and clearance. Check venue exhibition, online distribution and trailers against scope. [WIPO guide](../sources/bibliography.md#src-wipo-clear) · [CapCut terms](../sources/bibliography.md#src-cap-license) |
| AI disclosure in Kazakhstan | For synthetic results as defined in the law, Article 21(2) sets a distribution condition combining machine-readable marking and a warning perceivable by users. Article 22 and implementing rules address machine-readable forms. | Identify covered content and role, determine required implementation for the release, and check the exported and distributed file for the required marking and viewer-facing warning. [Official law](../sources/bibliography.md#src-kz-ai) · [Consolidated text](../sources/bibliography.md#src-kz-ai-cons) · [Implementing order](../sources/bibliography.md#src-kz-mr) |
| AI disclosure in the EU | Where Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 applies, Article 50(4) covers deployer disclosure for content constituting a deepfake. Evidently artistic, creative or fictional works have a tailored disclosure method that preserves enjoyment of the work. Article 50(2) separately addresses provider marking. | Check scope, current text, the generated or manipulated content, production role, disclosure format and delivery. Keep the screening and promotional uses in view. [EUR-Lex](../sources/bibliography.md#src-eu-ai50) |

## Release ledger

Store `asset_id`, origin or creator, tool and version if generated, input source, approved use, consent or license reference, intended distribution, disclosure treatment, owner and unresolved decision. Make a cue sheet for music. Attach a separate short AI-use statement showing where AI entered the workflow and which human choices shaped the film. Check that the statement and credits match the final cut.
