# Finished-film checklist

## Story and cut

- [ ] Check the finished cut against the user's premise and intended emotional experience.
- [ ] Confirm the central action or organizing idea, viewer information and ending are legible.
- [ ] Review the timing of holds, cuts, silence and reactions. Confirm that deliberate violations create the intended effect.
- [ ] Complete the [working rubric](evaluation_rubric.md) with evidence, and close critical findings.

## Image and sound

- [ ] Play the full rendered file. Check delivery crop, resolution, frame rate, codec/container, duration and absence of accidental frames or gaps.
- [ ] Review focus, visual artifacts, continuity, color, unexpected clipping and mismatched exposure around edits.
- [ ] Listen to the exported mix for sync, voice continuity, speech, fades, ambience, dynamics and unintended silence. Measure to the destination spec when required.
- [ ] Check title, credit names, subtitles, translation, timing, visibility and any separately delivered caption file.

## Rights and delivery

- [ ] Check the current destination's exact runtime definition, file and caption requirements, eligibility, rights warranties and process documentation.
- [ ] Resolve asset permissions, identifiable person or voice authorization, music and distribution scope. Check any provider-asset restrictions.
- [ ] Record AI tools and modes, generated or altered segments, provenance and human creative decisions.
- [ ] Apply required disclosure and marking. Check what survives export and upload in the actual release path.
- [ ] Keep the approved export, project version, editable stems, shot and cue lists and private records of the necessary permissions.

See [rights and disclosure](rights_and_disclosure.md). Treat an unresolved legal, consent or delivery requirement as a release gate independent of the artistic score.
