# Film festival working rubric

Use this 100-point rubric to review a finished 3–7 minute film. The five dimensions come from the project brief. The weights, anchors and readiness threshold are working choices for this rulebook. Festival organizers set their own rules. See [release rights and festival requirements](rights_and_disclosure.md).

## Score the film

Give each criterion an integer rating from 0 to 5. For each dimension multiply its weight by the rating and divide by five, then sum to a maximum of 100. Use 0 for absent or ineffective work supported by the viewed material, 1 for severe problems, 2 for uneven execution, 3 for effective work with material gaps, 4 for strong work with minor gaps, and 5 for the strongest anchor. Mark unreviewable evidence as pending and leave the total pending.

| Criterion | Weight | 1 · Major revision | 3 · Effective with gaps | 5 · Strong evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Artistic merit, narrative and direction | 30 | Actions and stakes are unclear or the film lacks a coherent organizing experience. | The central turn is legible, with some redundant scenes or underdeveloped choices. | Direction, performance and structure make consequential choices legible and sustained. Every major beat serves the film's intention. |
| Originality and variety in the use of AI tools | 15 | The use of tools yields generic or repetitive results with little relation to the idea. | The tools serve parts of the film, with some conventional or inconsistent choices. | The workflow and results use AI with purpose and range. The human creative contribution and the effect of the tools are apparent or evidenced. |
| Visual style, color grading and sound design | 20 | Visual and sonic decisions compete with the film or feel unconnected. | A coherent style is present, with weak contrasts, cues or transitions. | Image, color and sound express viewpoint, rhythm and thematic or emotional change. |
| Technical execution in image, sound and editing | 15 | Visible artifacts, continuity, intelligibility or export problems impede the work. | Most cuts and cues work, with some remaining defects that can be localized. | Shot joins, motion, synchronization, mix and delivery are clean where the story needs them. Deliberate ruptures read as intended. |
| Emotional impact and overall impression | 20 | The film leaves a vague or unintended emotional effect. | The intention is felt in several beats, with a less convincing transition or ending. | The film sustains a distinctive emotional experience and its final beat leaves a recognizable impression. |

Score the effect of aesthetic choices under style and their execution under technical quality. For example, an expressive off-screen cue belongs to style. A synchronization error in the same cue belongs to technical execution. Reward what AI use contributes to the work, including a purposeful single-tool workflow where suitable.

A proposed internal readiness gate is **75/100**, with at least **3/5** in both artistic merit and emotional impact. Critical continuity, rights, disclosure, eligibility or export blockers require a separate resolution. Record the reason if the user chooses a different editorial threshold.

## Stage reviews

| Stage | Evidence to examine | Pass check | Return work to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Screenplay | Brief, beat outline, script and timed read | Viewer can follow pursuit or organizing idea, escalation, choice and intended ending. Time and dialogue are credible. | Story or screenplay |
| Storyboard and shot list | Ordered panels, maps, frames, shot IDs, sound and animatic | Each shot has a job. Essential action, axis, staging and viewpoint read. Duration and transitions can be tested. | Direction, coverage or staging |
| Generated footage | Candidate takes at delivery crop, frame anchors, audio and adjacent clips | Story-critical action, identity, props, lighting and sync hold through the usable interval. Record exact trims and faults. | Frame, motion or sound |
| Rough cut | Assembled timeline, scene beds, rough mix and alternate cut points | Story and emotion survive the edit. Check continuity, pacing, bridges, intelligibility and consequences of discontinuities. | Edit, sound or upstream coverage |
| Finished film | Rendered export, destination spec, provenance and release record | Apply the five criteria, play the complete file, and close critical delivery and rights gates. | Owner of any open fix |

For each finding give criterion or gate, stage, affected ID, evidence and timecode, severity, fix owner, and recheck. Ask an uninstructed viewer what happened and what they felt before explaining the intent. Use [continuity checks](continuity_checklist.md) and the [finished-film checklist](final_film_checklist.md).
