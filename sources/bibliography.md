See the [machine-readable index](source_index.yaml), the [film-school comparison](../research/film_schools.md), and the [open questions](../research/open_questions.md). Source IDs are permanent. URLs point to the consulted edition or record.

Use curricula for public teaching objectives, original passages for attributed craft principles, and the specific study or guide for an empirical or technical claim. Publisher and library entries can identify a work and describe its scope. For indexed entries, the text available for inspection was the indexed preview. Date entries refer to the consulted edition, article, or original item as specified.
