# Bibliography and evidence policy

[Machine-readable source index](source_index.yaml)

A checked source is recorded with a stable ID and a statement of what its available content supports. The register distinguishes institution pages, primary texts, interviews, archival records, scholarly analysis, professional guides, publisher listings, and vendor documentation. Each type has a different scope. Record AI adaptations as rulebook inferences and identify any assumptions that still need testing.
