# 03 · Screenplay

## Inputs

Film brief, ordered beats and any required style or formatting constraints.

## Procedure

1. Write scene headings with interior/exterior, location and time. Use present-tense screen action and speaker cues and dialogue where needed.
2. Express intention in behavior, pauses and choices. Keep internal intent, subtext and beat IDs in separate notes when they are useful for direction.
3. Add a transition where it carries a storytelling purpose. Keep camera lists and generation syntax for technical planning. Time action and spoken lines.

## Output contract

Screenplay in Markdown or a production screenplay format. Each scene carries a scene_id in its accompanying manifest, a heading, action blocks, speaker/dialogue blocks as applicable, beat_ids and optional direction notes. Transitions are optional.

## Reusable prompt

> Turn the beat outline into a readable short screenplay. Include observable action, purposeful dialogue, and necessary sound or transitions. Keep subtext and intention in separate notes where helpful. Preserve the approved ending and flag any change you propose.

## Quality check

- Scene headings and speakers are unambiguous.
- The key action, outcome and emotional transition can be inferred on screen.
- A timed read and action estimate fit the budget.

## Revision trigger

Revise if exposition repeats the action, a necessary state change cannot be seen or heard, or a scene has no useful purpose.

## Handoff

Pass the screenplay, manifest and timed notes to breakdown. For screenplay layout see the [Academy format sample](../sources/bibliography.md#src-nicholl-format).
