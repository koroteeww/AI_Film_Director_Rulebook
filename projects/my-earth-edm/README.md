# My Earth EDM

A groundskeeper trying to dry a crowded square follows the water back to a spring. The path stays dry when she and a passerby give the water its own way through.

**FP-my-earth-edm · Narrative EDM music video · 4:00 target · 16:9 · directing draft v0.1**

Begin with the pressure of a route everyone has to cross. Bring the audience down to the height of a bare hand and let a small act of care change how the whole square moves. Each drop changes scale. The first is a held touch, the second follows moving water, and the final one opens the shared space. The lyric’s seasonal and celestial images enter as Mara’s associations with the same ground.

## Brief

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Author material | The supplied English lyrics move from city hustle to touching spring water and grass, then to the discovery that Earth is a living home shared by everyone. |
| Focal character | CH-001 · Mara, a groundskeeper |
| Playable goal | Clear the existing garden spillway so the spring feeds the roots beside the crossing. |
| Stakes | If she keeps sweeping the overflow away, people keep dodging it and the garden stays dry. |
| Dramatic question | Can Mara keep the walking route open and give the spring water back to the planted strip? |
| Tone | Restless, tactile, then expansive |
| Last image | The camera has risen far enough to see the walkway and the runnel at once. People go on their way and the water keeps moving beside them. |

The English lyric and section order come from the author. These are the proposed directing choices.

- Mara, Iman and the spring square are original proposals. The basin and garden spillway already exist.
- 16:9 landscape, no performance lip sync, one continuous morning in the square.
- The two seasonal details are associative inserts. The square remains in the same season.
