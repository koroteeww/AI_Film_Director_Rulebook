# The Second Witness

**Screenplay draft v0.1 · English · 5:20 target**

## SC-001 · EXT. PRIVATE OBSERVATORY TERRACE – NIGHT

*BT-001 · 0:00–0:44*

The stair door opens onto a narrow stone terrace. Warm light spills as far as the top step. Beyond the parapet, a ridge cuts a black line against the stars.

ELIAS SAYE stands at the observing bench. A red lamp lights his hands. Two pale points hold on his recorder.

ADRIAN VENN steps out carrying a sheet of cream paper. Before he clips it to the bench, he rolls Elias’s loose cuff back from the recorder. Elias lifts his wrist without looking. A signet ring catches the light. Under the house name, one word reads EXCLUSIVE.

**ADRIAN**  
The trustees wanted the announcement ready.

Elias slides the paper aside just far enough to reach his own slip.

**ELIAS**  
We've only got a prediction.

**ADRIAN**  
They vote on our budget Friday.

Elias takes a yellow pencil from his coat and rules a second line below his own name. He leaves it blank.

## SC-002 · EXT. OBSERVING BENCH – NIGHT

*BT-002 · 0:44–1:32*

Elias puts the pencil beside the recorder and points to its left light.

**ELIAS**  
This is the one that might go out. That one's my reference.

**ADRIAN**  
So we watch them.

**ELIAS**  
We do. I'd like someone with another telescope to watch as well.

He points down into the valley. A small square of light sits beneath the ridge.

**ELIAS**  
Nera's on the field. She needs the exact star.

**ADRIAN**  
That’s the night school?

Elias nods. Adrian keeps looking past the window, into the sky above it.

**ADRIAN**  
If there were someone looking back, what would you tell them about us?

**ELIAS**  
I'd ask them a question first.

A breath. Adrian looks at the notice, then at the radio.

## SC-003 · EXT. RADIO END OF THE BENCH – NIGHT

*BT-003 · 1:32–2:20*

Adrian rests a finger on the talk key. The amber pilot stays lit. He presses.

**ADRIAN**  
Valley station. Adrian Venn. Can you hear us?

A short hiss from the speaker.

**NERA (RADIO)**  
I've been here an hour.

Adrian looks over at Elias, who has the finder image ready to send.

**ADRIAN**  
Will you record with us?

**NERA (RADIO)**  
If you use our log, put the school's name on it. The students made the mount.

Adrian looks at the second line Elias drew.

**ADRIAN**  
All right.

Elias sends the finder image.

**ELIAS**  
Keep your time on your own sheet. We'll compare after.

The radio falls quiet.

## SC-004 · EXT. TELESCOPE BAY – NIGHT

*BT-004 · 2:20–3:20*

The telescope makes one small adjustment and stops.

Elias watches the recorder. Adrian starts to ask something, then takes his hand off the talk key. He waits beside his friend.

On the recorder, the two points hold.

The left point dims almost to nothing. The right point stays bright.

The left point returns.

Elias writes **21–23** on his slip. Adrian watches the pencil move.

**ELIAS**  
Valley. Read yours first.

A beat with just the night air.

**NERA (RADIO)**  
Down at twenty-one. Back at twenty-three. Reference held.

Adrian checks the figures on Elias's slip.

## SC-005 · EXT. RADIO END OF THE BENCH – NIGHT

*BT-005 · 3:20–4:20*

Adrian puts the unsigned notice beside the observation slip. They both fit in the red light.

**ADRIAN**  
What name do you use on the log?

**NERA (RADIO)**  
Nera Dos. Valley Night School.

Elias holds out the pencil. Adrian takes it. He draws a line through EXCLUSIVE and fills in the second line on the slip.

He lays the pencil across the two names. Adrian opens the guard on the radio selector. He sets it to OPEN. The pilot changes to pale blue.

**ADRIAN**  
Joint notice. Nera Dos at the Valley Night School and Elias Saye at Venn Observatory. We recorded the same dip.

He releases the key. The pilot stays blue.

**ELIAS**  
We'll send both logs.


## SC-006 · EXT. NORTH RAIL – NIGHT

*BT-006 · 4:20–5:12*

Adrian glances out from the bench and presses the talk key again.

**ADRIAN**  
Nera, where should I look?

**NERA (RADIO)**  
See our window below the ridge? Start just above it.

Adrian walks to the rail. Elias joins him. The radio is behind them now.

Adrian finds the square of light, then lets his eyes move up. His right hand settles open on the stone.

Beyond the same ridge, the field of stars holds.
