# The Second Witness

Minutes before a star may blink out, an astronomer needs a second telescope to check what his instrument sees. The only one ready belongs to a night school in the valley, and his aristocratic friend has promised his family the first announcement.

**FP-the-second-witness · Astronomical chamber drama · 5:20 target · 16:9 · directing draft v0.1**
