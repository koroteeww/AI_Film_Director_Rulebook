# Believe

As a night train takes Lena away from home, she keeps her parents on a video call to put off saying goodbye. A tunnel breaks the call. A gesture her mother made on the platform gives her somewhere else to look.

**FP-believe · Narrative music video · 3:36 target · 16:9 · directing draft v0.1**

Let the audience feel the grip of an unfinished goodbye, then see Lena loosen it. Bring the parents and daughter into the same composition across distance while keeping their spaces distinct. Every chorus returns to the same idea through a different image. First they share a place, then a screen, then a sky. In the final fade the image can hold after the people have lowered their hands.

## Brief

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Author material | The supplied English lyrics return to a shared sky, love and faith, then ask us to let the past go and find steadiness when the ground feels uncertain. |
| Focal character | CH-001 · Lena, a young adult leaving home |
| Playable goal | Keep her parents with her for the first minutes of the journey. |
| Stakes | A dropped call makes the departure feel final before she is ready to let go. |
| Dramatic question | When the call goes dark, can Lena stay with the goodbye she has been trying to put off? |
| Tone | Intimate, uncertain, then quietly devotional |
| Last image | Two separate edges frame the same moon. The phone stays face down, and the last image stays with Lena’s view from the moving carriage. |

The English lyric and section order come from the author. These are the proposed directing choices.

- Lena, Zoya, Pavel and the train departure are original proposals.
- 16:9 landscape, no sung lip sync or required dialogue, one evening on a short stretch of the journey.
- The crescent is a matched visual anchor for the same evening. Neither party can see the other after the train leaves.
