# Believe shot list

24 finished shots cover 3:30 of picture. The final 6 seconds are reserved for credits. The clock is a silent timing draft.

Plan each generated segment for its edited hold plus roughly 1 second of handle at each end. These planned segments run 10–11 seconds. Set the request duration and verify the available mode at the generation stage. Companion panels are alternate phases of a shot and add zero time to the cut.

Wide, medium and close labels describe the intended scale in 16:9. Lens figures are full-frame equivalent intent. The [structured project record](project.json) contains lens intent, angle, focus, motion path, blocking, light, color, sound and entry and exit state for each shot.
